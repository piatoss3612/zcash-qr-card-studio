One broad ribbon-like beam of refracted light curves only along the right edge, breaking into two or three simple flat color facets. Not watercolor, not fantasy painting, not a blueprint, not celestial-map linework, not ornate parchment.

Composition/framing: portrait ratio 1311:1819. Leave approximately 80–85% of the sheet as genuinely unprinted pearl paper. Preserve a broad calm upper-center and central area for a large payment QR. Keep the lower-left mostly unprinted for a small installation panel. Concentrate the observatory object at upper-left and the prism/telescope object at lower-right; crop them decisively at the page edges.
