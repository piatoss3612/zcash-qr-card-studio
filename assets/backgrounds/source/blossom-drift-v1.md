## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.

Create one original full-bleed A6 portrait background layer for a print-friendly QR card editor, using the supplied Indigo Wave background only as a reference for restraint, negative-space composition, and low ink density—not as imagery to copy.

Design an abstract cherry-blossom drift on warm ivory fibrous paper. Frame the page asymmetrically with a slender dark-indigo branch entering from one upper corner and a restrained current of small blossom silhouettes and loose petals flowing toward the opposite lower edge. Use sparse dusty blush pink blossoms, muted Vizor crimson accents, and fine indigo/navy linework. Keep the center and upper-center very calm, pale, low-contrast, and unobstructed for a large payment QR code. Keep the lower installation-information area quiet enough for small dark text and a secondary QR. Maintain large negative space and low total ink coverage suitable for event handouts.

The result must be only an opaque background image: no QR codes, no text, no logo, no border frame, no UI, no cats, no characters, no shadows, no guides, and no watermark. Avoid recognizable franchise or song imagery, anime/fan art, Japanese castles, torii gates, pagodas, flags, calligraphy, portraits, swords, dense floral wallpaper, grunge, scratches, stains, photorealism, gradients, or glossy effects. Original ornamental print design, refined pixel-adjacent line rhythm but not blocky pixel art. Portrait ratio approximately 0.72 (A6).
