## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: print-efficient full-bleed A6 portrait background layer for a static QR card editor

Primary request: create a restrained low-ink “Brass Rampart” background that is unmistakably fortress-themed through its hardware and printmaking language, while remaining subordinate to a payment QR and a pixel-art guardian cat.

Scene/backdrop: mostly unprinted warm off-white paper. A large cropped portcullis counterweight wheel and one short believable chain appear at the upper-left edge; a bold riveted shield rim enters from the lower-right corner; a low blocky crenellation silhouette runs only along part of the bottom edge; two or three isolated brass rivet heads connect the composition. All hardware must be structural, secular, and clearly recognizable.

Style/medium: hand-pulled two-color linocut / spot-color screenprint. Use bold simplified contours, intentional carved gaps, sparse cross-hatching, and slightly imperfect ink edges. Flat graphic forms, not watercolor, not a fantasy painting, not stationery decoration.

Composition/framing: portrait ratio 1311:1819, full bleed but with approximately 80–85% of the sheet left as unprinted paper. Preserve broad quiet space through the upper-center and center for a large payment QR. Keep the lower-left mostly unprinted for a small installation panel. The fortress objects should be asymmetric, cropped by the edges, and visually distinct without forming a decorative border around every side.

Color palette: only muted old-brass ochre and soft charcoal-brown ink on warm off-white paper, with an optional tiny oxblood accent on one leather strap. CMYK-friendly, moderate contrast, no large solid dark fills.

Materials/textures: suggested through sparse carved marks only; do not fill the paper with texture.

Print constraints: target low ink coverage around 15–20%; no full-page wash; no dark background; no gradients; no translucent fog; no broad shadows; no dense halftone field; no tiny hairline details.

Constraints: background only; no characters, cats, animals, people, weapons, heraldic emblems, religious symbols, text, letters, numbers, logos, QR codes, UI, card frames, mockups, watermarks, orbit lines, stars, planets, waves, beige watercolor wash, stains, splatters, thin filigree, floating hardware, or duplicated objects.
