Use case: a character-design option sheet for a new embed Vizorcat, a star-keeper scholar, so the user can choose one design. Draw exactly four full-body characters side by side on one landscape canvas (1536 × 1024), left to right 1–4, clearly separated with empty space between them, each turned toward screen-left.

The previous design (a plain gray cat in a pointed wizard hat and a starry robe) was rejected as generic and mass-produced. Each option must be a DIFFERENT cat with its own memorable identity — the way the print Samurai (a solid black cat with a healed scar over one closed eye) and the Classic Guardian are instantly recognisable — built from a natural cat coat, one permanent feature, and a distinctive outfit silhouette. No pointed wizard hats.

All four show the same chosen moment: tracing a constellation in the air toward screen-left with one raised open paw (pad side facing the viewer), a short trail of five tiny gold star dots joined by thin gold lines floating along the path, face full of wonder; the other paw raised open at chest height with the pad side facing the viewer, so both arms are clearly visible (never hide one arm).

1 — Old Sage: a smoke-gray longhaired cat with a big white cheek-and-chin ruff that reads like a beard, bushy brow whisker tufts and copper eyes; small round brass spectacles; a deep-indigo hooded mantle with the hood down, a short star-chart shoulder cape, and a satchel of rolled charts.
2 — Night Academic: a tortoiseshell cat whose face is split cleanly down the middle, one half black and one half ginger, with gold eyes; a flat square academic cap with a small gold star tassel; a dark high-collared cape dotted with tiny gold stars over a cream shirt, and a brass spyglass on the belt.
3 — Sleepy Night Owl: a white van-pattern cat with a cream-orange cap of color on the head and a cream-orange tail, sleepy half-lidded blue eyes; a long floppy midnight-blue nightcap dotted with stars and ending in a small pom; an oversized soft robe with rolled cuffs; a small round star lantern hanging from the belt.
4 — Spotted Stargazer: a silver spotted-tabby cat whose dark spots look like a scattered star map, with lynx-tip ear tufts and amber eyes; a short hooded capelet in navy with a ring of small brass dial buttons; a leather strap across the chest holding a folding brass star-dial.
