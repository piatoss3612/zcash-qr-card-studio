## Design and local visual review

The cream-and-seal cat wears a warm ivory bridal-mask-inspired face covering, blue-black short jacket and gathered trousers, ivory collar, burgundy knot and tassels, and carries one small dagger low beside the hip. The other paw adjusts the mask. Both ears, two forelimbs, two short legs, tail and dagger fit inside the image.

The mask is a fantasy interpretation rather than a replica of a specific Hahoe artifact. It has red cheeks and lips, arched brows and dark slanted eye openings. Its eye openings are darker and wider than the restrained slits requested. The face is covered, so underlying eye and muzzle fidelity cannot be verified from this concept. Raised paw looks relatively flat. The body remains compact but costume and texture are more elaborate than the bare model. These are review observations, not production identity certification.
