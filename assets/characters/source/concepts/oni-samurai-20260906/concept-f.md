## Visual review

The open amber eye is round again and the severe slanted lid and compressed brow have been removed. The permanent closed eye and healed scar remain recognizable. The result reads friendlier and less haughty; confidence comes mainly from the open paw and upright stance. A short open indigo jinbaori adds broad textile shapes, oxblood lining, gold edges and wave motifs on both hems. Its left panel lifts outward slightly. A single crimson tassel accents the naginata collar. The dark crimson sculpted oni mask, crescent kabuto, navy segmented armor and crimson waist cords remain. Both ears, two arms, two legs, complete tail and the entire single naginata are in frame, without additional weapons.

The body still reads longer and the texture finer than the approved model sheet, so identity/pixel-density fidelity is not approved for production. ## Historical reference

Nagoya Touken World describes a naginata said to have been used in battle by commander Ukita Hideie: https://www.meihaku.jp/ginga-sword/ginga-busho-sword/ . This supports the plausibility of a commander carrying a naginata; the attribution is presented as a tradition, not independently verified proof of the specific battle use. This artwork is a fictional costume design, not a reconstruction of that weapon or commander.
