## Visual review

The deep crimson sculpted half mask covers the muzzle and chin while leaving the scarred closed eye and single amber eye visible. The golden crescent, navy segmented armor, crimson cords, open presenting paw, gunbai, one sheathed katana and complete tail remain recognizable and inside the frame. Two main curved fangs read as gold rather than the requested aged ivory, and several smaller gold teeth were added. The mask reads larger and more expressive than the original simple hanbo; this is a review choice, not yet approved production art.

The generator returned an opaque RGB image with a baked checkerboard despite the transparent-output request. Alpha preparation, matte QA, editor scale measurement and live registration have not been performed. Do not use this concept directly as a live cutout.
