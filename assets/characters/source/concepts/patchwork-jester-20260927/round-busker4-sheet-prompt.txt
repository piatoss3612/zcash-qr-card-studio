Use case: a character-design option sheet for a new embed Vizorcat, the Patchwork Busker — a cheerful street performer whose whole outfit is patched together from mismatched cloth squares with visible stitches. Draw exactly two full-body characters side by side on one landscape canvas (1536 × 1024), left and right, clearly separated with wide empty space between them, each turned three-quarters toward screen-left with its head, gaze and main gesture toward screen-left.

Proportions come FIRST. Each busker must be built on the same chubby chibi body as the print Vizorcats in references 2–4 (Classic Guardian, Samurai, Grove Ranger, Wandering Swordsman):
- The head (chin to the top of the ears, without headwear) is as tall as the entire body below the chin. Head and body are each about half of the figure's height.
- The head is as wide as, or wider than, the shoulders; big round eyes.
- A short, round, stocky body; very short stubby legs in small round boots — the patchwork vest hem reaches almost to the boot tops.

Outfit for both: a floppy patchwork cap with a tassel (both ears out), a scarf, a short patchwork vest with a ragged hem, patched trousers and small worn boots, in faded green, rust, mustard, cream and denim-blue patches with stitched seams (reference 1 shows the patch style only). Beside each cat's feet on the ground sits its own empty upturned patched felt tip hat — empty, no coins, no sign, no text.

Left — Juggling busker: a black-and-white "patched" bicolor cat — only black and white fur, no orange or ginger anywhere — with irregular black patches on white, a small notch in the tip of one ear (a street cat's mark) and green eyes. It juggles three small patchwork beanbag balls that float in a neat arc in the air above its paws toward screen-left (no paw touches a ball); both paws raised open at chest height, pad sides facing the viewer; a proud, delighted grin toward screen-left.
Right — Harmonica busker: a solid cream (buff) cat with a freckled pink nose and amber eyes. A small harmonica held at its mouth by a thin wire neck rack (no paw touches it), cheeks clearly puffed round as it blows into the harmonica toward screen-left; both paws raised beside the shoulders clapping the beat, pad sides facing the viewer; body turned three-quarters toward screen-left.

References (attached in this order):
1. A crop of patched trousers and a ragged hem — use ONLY as the patch style (squares, visible stitches, ragged hem).
2. The Vizorcat proportion reference — the body proportions to match exactly.
3–4. The print Samurai and Classic Guardian — the finished drawing style (outline weight, stepped pixel-art-adjacent edges, cel shading, rich detail) and body proportions.
5. The approved Vizorcat paw sheet.

For both: no calico or tortoiseshell coats; both arms clearly visible, never one-armed; both feet visible on the ground. Every visible paw has exactly four toe beans (two at the top, one lower on each side) around one palm pad, like panel A of the paw sheet, drawn large and clearly readable; no paw grips anything; no fingers, thumbs or knuckles. Exactly two cat ears, each drawn once at normal length, coming up through or in front of the cap; nothing else looks like an ear. No stringed instruments, no coins. Non-sexual chibi animal mascot. No text, letters, numbers, runes, religious or occult symbols, logos, QR codes, frames, motion lines, music notes, ground shadow or scenery.
Background: transparent alpha if available; otherwise one perfectly flat pure #00FF00 chroma-key green. One image.
