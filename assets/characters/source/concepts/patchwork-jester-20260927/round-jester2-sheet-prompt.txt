Use case: a character-design option sheet for a new embed Vizorcat, the Patchwork Jester — a cheeky, lovable clown whose whole outfit is sewn from big bold patches. Draw exactly three full-body variations of the SAME character side by side on one landscape canvas (1536 × 1024), left to right 1–3, clearly separated with wide empty space between them, each turned three-quarters toward screen-left with its head and gaze toward screen-left.

Proportions come FIRST. The jester must be built on the same chubby chibi body as the print Vizorcats in references 2–4 (Classic Guardian, Samurai, Grove Ranger, Wandering Swordsman):
- The head (chin to the top of the ears, without the hood points) is as tall as the entire body below the chin — a BIG head. Head and body are each about half of the figure's height; the body is not taller than the head.
- The head is about 1.2 times as wide as the shoulders; big round eyes.
- A short, round, stocky body; very short stubby legs with small round feet.

The patchwork look (critical — match reference 1 exactly in style): every garment is made of BIG, bold, irregular squares and rectangles of cloth — each patch about a quarter of the hood's width — in warm orange, charcoal black, cream and faded olive green, joined by thick, clearly visible cream cross-stitches along every seam. Edges end in ragged, fringed strips that hang loose.

The character: a black-and-white "patched" bicolor cat — only black and white fur, irregular black patches on white, no orange or ginger fur anywhere — with a round black patch of fur around its left eye like a natural eye-patch marking, and bright green eyes. Both ears are normal, smooth and unnotched. Outfit: a floppy two-pointed patchwork jester hood with a cream pom-pom on each point and both cat ears coming out through it; a short patchwork cape whose hem hangs in ragged strips; a patchwork tunic with a small ruffled cream collar; patched puffy shorts; striped stockings and soft pointed patchwork shoes. A cheerful, cheeky clown — friendly, never scary; no face paint, no red nose.

Three different moments:
1 — Ta-da flourish: both paws raised beside the face in open "jazz hands", pad sides facing the viewer, a huge cheeky grin toward screen-left.
2 — Balancing act: standing with both feet on top of a small patchwork ball, knees slightly bent, both paws raised beside the shoulders for balance (elbows bent, pad sides facing the viewer), tongue poking out in concentration.
3 — Mischief: leaning toward screen-left on its toes, both paws raised together beside the screen-left side of its face as if whispering a secret, pad sides facing the viewer, one eye winking.

References (attached in this order):
1. Crops of an earlier patchwork concept (the face and instrument are erased) — the patch style to match: big patches, thick cross-stitches, ragged strip hem. Do not copy its coat colours.
