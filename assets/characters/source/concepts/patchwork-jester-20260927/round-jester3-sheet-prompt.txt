Draw exactly three full-body variations of the SAME character side by side on one landscape canvas (1536 × 1024), left to right 1–3, clearly separated with wide empty space between them, each turned three-quarters toward screen-left with its head and gaze toward screen-left.

Proportions come FIRST. The jester must be built on the same chubby chibi body as the print Vizorcats in references 2–4 (Classic Guardian, Samurai, Grove Ranger, Wandering Swordsman):

The character: a black-and-white "patched" bicolor cat — only black and white fur, irregular black patches on white, no orange or ginger fur anywhere — with a round black patch of fur around its left eye like a natural eye-patch marking, and bright green eyes. A cheerful, cheeky clown — friendly, never scary; no face paint, no red nose.
