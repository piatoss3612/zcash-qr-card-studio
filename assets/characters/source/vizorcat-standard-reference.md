# Vizorcat Standard Base model sheet

Create the canonical bare identity model sheet that every Vizorcat Variant must inherit.

Reference roles:

- OG Gray is the authoritative source for the face personality, compact chibi scale, gray-and-white identity, large golden-yellow eyes, and chunky pixel-art-adjacent rendering. Remove all Theme elements rather than redesigning the cat beneath them.
- One-eyed Boss is a sheet-layout and bare upright-pose reference only: four full-body views across the top and five front-facing expression portraits across the bottom. Do not copy its black fur, closed eye, scar, stocky bulk, or commanding personality.

Canonical character: a bare adult-neutral chibi gray-and-white short-haired cat derived from OG Gray. Give it a rounded feline head with softly squared cheeks, medium triangular ears rooted at the same stable positions, two large round golden-yellow eyes, a small pink triangular nose, a short centered white muzzle, a tiny simple cat mouth, and three short dark cheek marks contained inside each cheek rather than long detached whiskers. Use medium neutral-gray fur on the crown, ears, back, outer limbs, and most of the tail; use white on the muzzle, chin, compact chest patch, paw tips, and tail tip. Keep markings simple, bilateral, and easy to reproduce.

Canonical proportion grid: keep the full-body top-of-ear-to-feet height compact and identical across all four views, approximately the approved One-eyed Boss height on the same 1536x1024 sheet. The head occupies about 46–50% of full-body height. The torso is a short simple rounded rectangle about 56–62% of head width, with no narrow waist. Hind legs are very short; paws are simple rounded feline blocks. The medium-thick tail curves outward without dominating the body.

Canonical pose: stand upright on two hind legs with a vertical torso, raised hips, two hind feet directly under the body, and short forepaws hanging neutrally at the sides without touching the floor. No sitting, long humanoid legs, stretched torso, muscular anatomy, hip shift, or contrapposto.

Canonical face grid: preserve the same skull outline, cheek width, ear-root position, eye diameter, eye centers, muzzle width, nose position, and mouth position across every view and expression. The eyes are large but leave a stable forehead and cheek margin; do not enlarge them into a different character style. Side profiles use the same short muzzle projection and head volume. The back view shows the same ear roots, skull width, coat split, and tail.

Layout on one perfectly flat #FF00FF chroma-key canvas:

- Top row: exactly four separated full-body neutral standing turnarounds in fixed order: front, facing screen-right, back, facing screen-left.
- Bottom row: exactly five separated front-facing head-and-shoulders expressions in fixed order: neutral, slight smile, firm/stern focus, happy/laughing, surprised.

Expression grammar:

- neutral: both golden eyes open, relaxed brows, tiny centered cat mouth
- slight smile: same eye scale, subtly lifted cheeks and mouth corners
- stern: pupils and eye scale unchanged, brows lowered slightly, small closed mouth
- happy: both eyes close into upward arcs, open wholesome cat smile
- surprised: both eyes open at the canonical size with slightly raised brows and a small round mouth; do not enlarge the skull or eyes

Rendering lock: chunky pixel-art-adjacent mascot illustration with a consistent stepped dark outline, controlled pixel clusters, limited gray/white/pink/gold palette, and three simple tonal levels: base, shadow, highlight. No painterly fur, airbrushed realism, dense texture, excessive dithering, glossy 3D rendering, or style changes between views.

Presentation lock: unmistakably feline, adult-neutral, wholesome, and non-sexual. No human secondary sexual characteristics, chest/bust definition, narrow waist, hip or pelvis emphasis, buttocks, muscular limbs, suggestive posture, flirtatious eyes, blush, tongue gesture, or gender coding.

Theme boundary: completely bare cat with no clothing, collar, armor, helmet, cape, belt, badge, bag, tool, weapon, prop, symbols, or accessories.

No text, labels, grids, visible cell borders, arrows, scenery, floor, cast shadows, glow, detached effects, stray pixels, or extra characters. The #FF00FF background must be uniform with no gradient or texture.
